Guide · For creators

Fees and who gets what

There are two fees. The platform fee is fixed and the same for every launch. Each creator sets their own creator fee when they launch. Both are only taken if a sale sells out.

WhoGets
The platform5% of the ETH the sale keeps, after any price differences have been set aside for buyers
The creatorThe creator fee: a share of the liquidity reserve, in tokens, set at launch between 0% and 20% of the reserve
The poolThe remaining 95% of the ETH and the rest of the reserve tokens
BuyersExactly the tokens they bought, plus any ETH owed back from their batch's final price. Fees never reduce either.

The creator fee

The creator picks their creator fee when they launch. It's a share of the liquidity reserve, the half of the supply held back for the pool, paid in tokens when the sale sells out. It can't be changed after launch.

The app rates every creator fee by the share of supply it takes: up to 5% is Healthy, up to 7.5% is High, and anything above is Very high. The creator sees the rating on the slider when they launch, and buyers see it on the sale page. These shares assume a 1x launch price multiplier. Above that, part of the reserve is burned, so the same fee is a bigger share of what's left, and the rating goes up with it.

The tokens come out of the pool's half, never out of what buyers bought. A higher creator fee means fewer tokens in the pool, which also lifts the price trading opens at. The pool at sellout explains that.

The creator gets no ETH

Worth saying plainly: launching doesn't pay you in ETH. Your share is your creator fee, in tokens, which you receive when the sale sells out. Everything else goes into the market or the platform fee.

If the sale doesn't sell out

No fees are taken at all. Buyers withdraw exactly what they paid.

Other costs

There may be a fee for creating a token. If there is, the launch form shows it above the launch button before you sign. Network gas applies to every transaction, as it does anywhere on-chain, and the app shows an estimate before you sign.
