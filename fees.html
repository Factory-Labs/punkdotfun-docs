Guide · For launchers

Fees and who gets what

Fees are fixed in the contract and the same for every launch. They're only taken if a sale sells out.

WhoGets
The platform5% of the ETH raised
The launcher5% of the liquidity reserve, which is 2.5% of the total supply, in tokens
The poolThe remaining 95% of the ETH and the remaining reserve tokens
BuyersExactly the tokens they committed for. Fees never reduce them.

The launcher gets no ETH

Worth saying plainly: launching doesn't pay you in ETH. Your share is 2.5% of the supply in tokens, which you receive when the sale sells out. Everything else goes into the market or the platform fee.

If the sale doesn't sell out

No fees are taken at all. Buyers withdraw exactly what they paid.

Other costs

There may be a fee for creating a token. If there is, the launch form shows it before you sign. On the alpha it's free. Network gas applies to every transaction, as it does anywhere on-chain, and the app shows an estimate before you sign.
