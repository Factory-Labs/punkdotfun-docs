FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people ask most.

General

What is punk.fun in one sentence?

A token launchpad that sells half a token's supply in a batched auction and turns the proceeds into a live market, or refunds everyone if it doesn't sell out.

Do I need to understand smart contracts?

No. You use the app and the contracts handle the rest.

What's PUNK?

The analytical side of punk.fun. It's coming soon. Everything live today is FUN. See PUNK.

For buyers

Why didn't I get my tokens straight away?

Buying commits ETH and locks in your share. You claim the tokens once the sale sells out. It works the same for everyone.

Why does the price keep changing?

Each batch opens at a new, usually higher price, and inside a batch the price can drift down if the launcher set a decay. See Pricing.

Can I end up paying more than I expected?

Not by default. Your buy is capped at the open batch's price, so if the batch sells out before your buy lands, it's refused instead. If you tick Buy at any price, it fills at whatever the next batch costs.

What if I buy more than a batch has left?

You get what's left of the batch and the extra ETH comes straight back in the same transaction.

What happens if the sale never sells out?

After the deadline, every buyer withdraws exactly the ETH they paid. There's no time limit on withdrawing, and no pool is created. See When a sale doesn't sell out.

Can I get a refund after a sale sells out?

No. Once it sells out the ETH goes into the pool. Refunds only exist for sales that miss their deadline.

How long does a sale run?

Between 1 and 30 days, set by the launcher, or until it sells out if that's sooner.

What price does trading open at?

The average price paid across the whole sale, unless the launcher set a launch price multiplier. Later batches pay more than the average, so they can start below what they paid. See The pool at sellout.

Why did my token estimate change?

In a batch-average sale, the amount is an estimate until your batch closes. See Batch-average pricing.

Is there a deadline to claim?

No.

Where can I see everything I'm owed?

Your Profile page lists anything you can claim or withdraw, plus your open positions in live sales.

For launchers

How much of my token gets sold?

Half. The other half seeds the pool, minus your 2.5%.

Do I set the fees or where the liquidity goes?

No. Fees, the split and the liquidity are fixed by the protocol. You set the sale rules: price, batch sizes, decay, length, start, pricing mode and the launch price multiplier.

What do I get?

2.5% of the supply in tokens when the sale sells out. You don't receive ETH from the sale.

Can I sell my 2.5%?

Yes, once you've received it. Buyers can see it on every sale page before they buy.

How many transactions does launching take?

One signature if your wallet can batch transactions. Otherwise four confirmations, and you can pick up where you left off if one is interrupted.

What if my sale doesn't sell out?

Buyers are refunded in full, no pool is created and no fees are taken.

Can the supply be inflated after launch?

No. The token has a fixed supply with no mint function and no owner. The supply can only go down, if you set a launch price multiplier above 1x.

Fees, risk and safety

What fees are there?

5% of the ETH raised to the platform and 2.5% of supply to the launcher, both only at sellout. There may be a fee for creating a token, shown before you sign. Gas applies as usual. See Fees and who gets what.

Who controls the liquidity after the sale?

Nobody. The LP tokens go to a dead address, so the liquidity can't be withdrawn.

Is buying a newly launched token safe?

No token launch is safe. New tokens are volatile, and trading opens at the sale's average price, so a late batch can be down straight away. punk.fun makes the mechanics fair and visible. It can't make a token a good investment.
