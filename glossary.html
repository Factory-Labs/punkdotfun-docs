Glossary

The terms you'll see on punk.fun and in these docs, in plain English.

TermWhat it means
AuctionThe sale for a launch. It runs in batches at rising prices.
BatchA block of tokens sold at its own price, shown as B1, B2 and so on. When one sells out, the next opens.
BuyA purchase at the current price. It fills straight away. There's no bidding and nobody can outbid you.
ClaimCollecting the tokens you bought, and any ETH you're owed back, once the sale has sold out.
Claimable ETHETH owed back to you after a sellout because you paid more than your batch's final price. Not the same as a refund.
CommitWhat a buy does: you send ETH now and claim tokens later.
CreatorThe person who launches a token and its sale.
Creator feeThe creator's share of the liquidity reserve, paid in tokens at sellout. Set at launch between 0% and 20% of the reserve, which is 0% to 10% of supply. The app shows it as Creator Fee, a share of supply rated Healthy, High or Very high.
Creator pageA public page listing everything one wallet has launched. The address under a token's name links to it.
DeadlineWhen buying stops if the sale hasn't sold out. Set at launch, between 4 hours and 30 days.
DecayHow much the price drops each block inside a batch, down to the price floor. On by default.
Eligibility checkA one-off confirmation on your first visit that you're allowed to use punk.fun. You're asked again if the Terms change.
FDVFully diluted value: the token's price times its full 1 billion supply, shown in dollars as a rough guide.
Final priceThe price a batch sold out at. Everyone who bought in that batch pays it.
Fixed supply1 billion tokens, and no way to mint more.
Launch price multiplierAn optional setting that opens the pool above the average sale price by burning some of the pool's tokens. 1x to 3x.
Launcher FeeWhat the Terms of Use call the creator fee.
Liquidity reserveThe half of the supply held back to seed the pool.
LP tokensThe tokens that represent ownership of the pool. On punk.fun they go to a dead address, so nobody owns the pool.
Platform fee5% of the ETH the sale keeps, taken at sellout.
Pool opening priceThe price trading starts at: roughly the average price across the sale, nudged by the creator fee and multiplied by the launch price multiplier.
Price floorThe lowest price decay can take a batch to. Set by the creator at launch.
Price limitA choice on the buy panel. This batch caps your buy at the open batch's price. Any batch lets it fill in the next one if this one sells out first.
RefundGetting back exactly the ETH you paid when a sale misses its deadline.
Sale allocationThe half of the supply sold to buyers.
Sanctions screeningEvery wallet is checked against sanctions lists when it connects. A listed wallet is disconnected.
SettlementWhat happens at sellout: fees taken, pool created, claims opened. All inside the final buy.
Trading poolThe Uniswap market created at sellout.
