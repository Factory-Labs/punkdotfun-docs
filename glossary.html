Glossary

The terms you'll see on punk.fun and in these docs, in plain English.

TermWhat it means
AuctionThe sale for a launch. It runs in batches at rising prices.
BatchA block of tokens sold at its own price. The app also calls it a tranche, shown as T1, T2 and so on. When one sells out, the next opens.
BuyA purchase at the current price. It fills straight away. There's no bidding and nobody can outbid you.
Buy at any priceAn option on the buy panel that removes the price cap, so your buy goes through even if the batch sells out first.
ClaimCollecting the tokens you bought, once the sale has sold out.
Claimable ETHETH owed back to you after a sellout because you paid more than your batch's final price. Not the same as a refund.
Creator feeThe launcher's share of the liquidity reserve, paid in tokens at sellout. Set at launch between 0% and 20% of the reserve, which is 0% to 10% of supply. The app calls it Launcher Fee.
CommitWhat a buy does: you send ETH now and claim tokens later.
DeadlineWhen buying stops if the sale hasn't sold out. Set at launch, between 1 and 30 days.
DecayHow much the price drops each block inside a batch, down to the minimum price.
Final priceThe price a batch sold out at. Everyone who bought in that batch pays it.
Fixed supply1 billion tokens, and no way to mint more.
Launch price multiplierAn optional setting that opens the pool above the average sale price by burning some of the pool's tokens. 1x to 3x.
LauncherThe person running the launch.
Launcher feeWhat the app calls the creator fee.
Liquidity reserveThe half of the supply held back to seed the pool.
LP tokensThe tokens that represent ownership of the pool. On punk.fun they go to a dead address, so nobody owns the pool.
Max priceThe most you'll pay per token on a buy. By default it's the open batch's price.
Minimum priceThe floor the price never decays below.
Platform fee5% of the ETH the sale keeps, taken at sellout.
Pool opening priceThe price trading starts at: roughly the average price across the sale, nudged by the creator fee and multiplied by the launch price multiplier.
RefundGetting back exactly the ETH you paid when a sale misses its deadline.
Sale allocationThe half of the supply sold to buyers.
SettlementWhat happens at sellout: fees taken, pool created, claims opened. All inside the final buy.
Trading poolThe Uniswap market created at sellout.
