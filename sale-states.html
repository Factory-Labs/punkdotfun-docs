Sale states

Every sale shows a badge next to its name. Here's what each one means and what you can do.

BadgeWhat it meansWhat you can do
SoonFunded and scheduled. Buying hasn't opened yet.Wait, or set a reminder for when it opens.
LiveBuying is open. The price follows the batch rules.Buy.
ClaimSold out. The pool is live.Claim your tokens, then trade.
UnsoldThe deadline passed without a sellout and nobody has withdrawn yet.Withdraw what you paid. The first withdrawal also closes the sale.
ExpiredThe sale has been closed and refunds are open.Withdraw exactly what you paid, whenever you like.
Not fundedThe launcher started a launch but didn't finish it, so the sale never opened.Nothing to buy or claim. Only the launcher can finish it.
SettlingSold out, pool not created yet. Settlement normally happens inside the final buy, so you shouldn't see this.Nothing. It's a safety state.
