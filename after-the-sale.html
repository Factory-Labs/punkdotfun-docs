Guide · For buyers

Claiming and after the sale

When a sale sells out, everything below happens inside that final buy. Nobody has to press anything except to claim their own tokens.

  1. Price differences are set asideETH owed back to buyers who paid more than their batch's final price is held for them to collect.
  2. Fees are taken5% of the remaining ETH goes to the platform, and the launcher's creator fee is paid in reserve tokens.
  3. The pool is seededThe remaining ETH and reserve tokens are paired in a Uniswap pool.
  4. The liquidity is locked for goodThe pool's LP tokens are sent to a dead address that nobody controls, so the liquidity can never be withdrawn.
  5. Claims openBuyers can collect their tokens, and any ETH they're owed back.

Claiming

One transaction claims all your tokens. There's no deadline.

Collecting ETH back

If you paid more than your batch's final price, which only happens when a sale has price decay, you're owed the difference in ETH. You collect it with its own transaction, separate from claiming your tokens. There's no deadline for this either. Pricing explains how it's worked out.

Your Profile page lists everything you can claim or withdraw across all sales, plus your open positions in live ones.

Trading

Once you've claimed, you can trade the token in its Uniswap pool. The sale page has a Trade on Uniswap button that takes you there. Trading opens at roughly the average price paid across the sale, not the last batch's price. The pool at sellout explains what that means for you.

The liquidity

No one holds the pool position: not the launcher, not punk.fun. It can't be withdrawn, staked or moved, and there are no rewards attached to it.

What "unruggable" covers

It means the pool's liquidity can't be pulled. It doesn't mean the price can't fall. Anyone holding tokens can sell them into the pool, including the launcher with their creator fee.

What "done" looks like

  • The token has a live pool and anyone can trade it.
  • Buyers can claim whenever they like, then trade.
  • The launcher has nothing left to do.
