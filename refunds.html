Guide · For buyers

When a sale doesn't sell out

A sale that doesn't sell out isn't left hanging. Every sale has a deadline, and after it everyone gets their money back in full.

The deadline

Every sale has one, set by the creator at launch between 4 hours and 30 days. You can see the time left on the sale page. Buying stops when it passes.

Getting your ETH back

Open the sale and press Withdraw on the Your Refund card. You get back exactly what you paid. Not a share, not minus a fee: the same amount. There's no deadline to withdraw, so there's no rush.

The first withdrawal closes the sale

Once the deadline has passed, the sale shows as Ended - ETH to claim. The first person to withdraw also closes the sale for everyone, which costs them a little extra gas. If your wallet can batch transactions, that's one confirmation. Otherwise it takes two: one to close the sale and one to send your ETH back. After that, everyone else just withdraws.

Your Profile page also lists anything you can withdraw, so you don't have to remember which sales you were in.

What happens to the tokens

No trading pool is created, no fees are taken, and no tokens go to buyers. Once the sale has been closed, the tokens can be taken back by the sale's owner, normally the wallet that started it. There's no button for that in the app yet.

Once a sale sells out, there are no refunds

The refund path only exists for sales that miss their deadline. As soon as a sale sells out, the ETH goes into the pool and your tokens are there to claim. The only ETH you can get back after a sellout is the difference between what you paid and your batch's final price. See Pricing.
